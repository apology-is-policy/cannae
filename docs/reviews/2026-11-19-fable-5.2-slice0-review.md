## The most important points

### P1. The verdict stands. It is one result seen eight times, and it was visible at the central values before the freeze.

The counts are right, and no measurement fault I found could turn 0 of 200 into 20 of 200. But the report presents eight cells and four failing patterns. The evidence is one shared stack, 200 draws, and two facts: the first side breaks about 20 s after the fronts meet, and the break starts at the front.

Evidence:
- I checked section 1 against `docs/slice0/scored/score-field-r0.txt:3-15` and `docs/slice0/scored/fails.txt:1-39, 197-235`, section 4 against `rungs.txt`, section 6 against `leaders.txt` and `open-flank.txt`, and the all-eight lines of `two.txt`. All agree.
- `docs/slice0/preregistration.md:82-94`: draw *i* is the same in every cell. `docs/rival/07-hazards.md:170-172`: on `base` the rungs are the same battle until the first man turns or falls. So the rung columns of `docs/slice0/report/01-verdict.md:54-61` are near copies.
- Patterns 1, 2, 6, the failing half of 4 and most of 7 are the same short fight. One hit on a side of 20 files in 20 s is 9 hits per file-hour against a band top of 2.0 (`crates/cannae-harness/src/metrics.rs:694-705`), so pattern 7 cannot pass on a run under about 90 s. Pattern 2 needs three gaps in a sector and two ten-second bins (`metrics.rs:483-534`).
- `docs/slice0/priors-field.md:26-29`: before the freeze, at the centres, a side broke in 128 of 128 runs at a median of 32 s "with almost no one down", the Romans in 14 of 16 seeds. Lines 39-52: Varro did the sum that explains it and wrote "I chose the centres without doing that sum". - `docs/slice0/preregistration.md:353-358` and `docs/rival/02-order-and-contact.md:105-125`: the frozen text itself records that the nominal Roman line breaks before the onset is spent.

Do: say this in the report. Next time no batch starts until an unscored pilot shows the central set can pass.

### P2. The line breaks because of the fast layer and one rule of the bout resolver. Memory is not involved, so rung 4 cannot mend it.

The report says the result "does not say which mechanism is wrong" (`01-verdict.md:108-111`). The code and the builder's own sum narrow it a good deal.

Evidence:
- A parried blow is read as the start of flight. A strike that does not wound drives a steady man back with chance 0.1 to 0.5 (`docs/slice0/priors-shared.md:125`) and puts him in posture `Back` (`crates/cannae-core/src/bouts.rs:379-382`), with the same two exits as a man who gave ground from fear (`crates/cannae-man/src/lib.rs:484-500`). While there he is alarm to all who see him and support to none (`lib.rs:290-293, 341-351`). At the centres about half the Roman front rank is in it at any time (`priors-field.md:44-50`). Varro called this "the first candidate" for a second round (`priors-field.md:55-62`). It is in neither the report nor the list of 26.
- The defender has no response but fear. Urge has no term for being attacked (`lib.rs:400-427`); hastati ardour is fixed at 0 (`priors-field.md:166-169`); an enemy stepping forward in front of him is alarm (`lib.rs:311, 317`). The tempo figure pools both sides' front ranks (`metrics.rs:728-736`), and its medians of 0.47 to 0.49 sit at the 0.5 that one side always forward and the other never would give. That split by side is my inference; no output prints it.
- Every coupling is positive and fast. A turned man in view is one unit, up to 8, against a margin of 5.75 (`rival/07-hazards.md:38-48, 87-89`), counted by side and not by body (`lib.rs:283-289`). Nerve varies between men by 0.5 to 1.25 units (`priors-field.md:165`). By vision 04's own theory (`docs/vision/04-psyche.md:238-246`) coupling that far above the disorder snaps in one avalanche.
- "Reaction time" is a cap on a hazard, not a delay (`lib.rs:459`; `priors-field.md:126`), and every man decides on the same 250 ms step (`crates/cannae-core/src/schedule.rs:8-11`). Among thirty onlookers someone answers at the next step.
- The line goes with hardly anyone hurt: hits per file-hour are under the band, which at this length means none, in 106 of 200 pairwise sets and 88 of 200 field sets (`fails.txt:33, 38, 229, 234`).
- Every rung adds alarm and none subtracts it (`crates/cannae-man/src/memory.rs:124-133`; `crates/cannae-gauges/src/lib.rs:117-131`). Rung 4 as designed adds leaning and a refractory spell on the same visible layer (`docs/slice0/rungs-3-4.md:82-91`).

Do: do not build rung 4. Replay a sample with the event log's causes (the genealogy of rival 01 §8, which the report never used) and count what put the first men of each collapse into `Back` and what tipped them. Then rework the fast layer: a knock-back that is not a fright, giving ground as a state a line can stay in, a defender who fights, a real latency, alarm counted by body.

### P3. Time to break is exponential in the margin, so the duration band under a 10 % rule could hardly be passed by any model of this form. The case for a slow clock is strengthened by argument only.

Evidence (the arithmetic is mine, not measured):
- One unit of alarm multiplies the hazard 4 to 8 times (`priors-field.md:107`; `rival/07-hazards.md:18-25, 133`). The pattern 6 band lets a constant hazard pass for a mean of 47 to 87 minutes (`preregistration.md:169-177`): a factor of 1.85, about 0.36 of a unit at the centre. Five minutes to two hours is 1.8 units. The priors move the margin by several: nerve's mean spans 1 unit, the flank weight 1 to 2.5 for each of up to three enemies (`priors-field.md:97, 164-165`).
- Both designs had said duration is "close to an assumed input", to be scored by direction (`docs/rival-comparison.md:60-62`; `preregistration.md:292-293`). Patterns 1 and 6 then band its value (`preregistration.md:133, 138`).
- The fast terms outweigh the slow ones. One neighbour's fall or lapse moves a man 2 to 5 units; *shaken* moves him 2.25 at most. Breath reaches only the urge to go forward (`lib.rs:421-425`; `rival/07-hazards.md:190`). So a line breaks at the first onset or outlasts it for long.
- The outputs show that shape. At field rungs 0 and 1 at least 25 sets had two or more seeds still fighting at twenty minutes (`docs/slice0/report/05-relief.md:19-39`; `score-field-r0.txt:30`), yet pattern 6 passes in one, nearly always because other seeds of the same sets broke early (`fails.txt:224-227`). The Weibull shape's lower quartile is 0.66 (`score-field-r0.txt:32`). The hazard falls with time; the ladder was built to separate hazards that rise.

This supports vision 04's claim that something slow must time the break (`04-psyche.md:200-204`) and tells against the rival's bet (`docs/rival/01-the-man.md:104-108`). No slow variable was tested on a line that held.

Do: calibrate duration in a first history-matching wave, as `docs/vision/07-validation.md:288-308` prescribes, and score the other patterns where a line holds. Define the knife-edge on the few parameters a screening finds, not as a share of a 101-line box.

### P4. Pattern 3 fails for a structural reason known beforehand, and its statistic has two faults.

Evidence:
- Structure. A man sees five by five cells of 2 m (`crates/cannae-core/src/view.rs:15-18`). A rear-rank Roman, 12.6 m behind his front, is dormant with every hazard at zero until someone within 4 to 6 m wavers (`lib.rs:431-437`; `rival/07-hazards.md:91-95`). Varro wrote so beforehand (`priors-field.md:289-291`). Neither design's mechanism was built: not the vision's sound and rumour (`04-psyche.md:98-117`), not the rival's wounded men coming back (`rival/01-the-man.md:76-78`; left out at `rival/07-hazards.md:213`).
- Fault one: ties go to the front rank. The first tenth is taken after sorting (tick, man number) (`metrics.rs:597-603`). Men are numbered rank by rank from the front (`crates/cannae-harness/src/scenario.rs:355-356`), and every turn of a man step carries one tick (`schedule.rs:18`). I estimate a bias of 0.05 to 0.1 in depth. I did not measure it, and it cannot carry 0.18-0.26 to 0.6.
- Fault two: it measures the wrong moment. Vision 03 has rear men slip away first, after which the front loses support and a gap opens (`docs/vision/03-formations-tactics.md:123-125`). The statistic asks who begins the cascade and discards earlier leavers as stragglers (`preregistration.md:155-163`). A model doing what 03 describes could fail it. Here the trickle does not exist either: the median count of stragglers is 0 (`docs/slice0/report/03-reported.md:38-39`).

So the result contradicts rival 01's "needs no rumour field" as built and leaves the vision's mechanism untested.

Do: recount from replays with ties shared, report the stragglers' depth, and re-specify the pattern as the depth of men who leave before the break.

### P5. The scenario carries first-order weight and was never varied, and the report does not say who lost.

Evidence:
- `scenarios/patch/base.toml:14-58`: the fronts start 7.5 m apart and the warband comes on at t = 0. No approach, no missiles, the Romans standing to receive. The battle is the onset.
- The warband stands at 1.0 m a file against 1.8 m, on about 20 m of a 36 m Roman front. Under this man density is morale and lethality at once: more enemies in reach to count as alarm (`rival/07-hazards.md:43-45`), two-on-one multipliers of 1.5 to 3 (`priors-shared.md:118`), a bout rate that belongs to each attacker (`rival/07-hazards.md:181-185`). Vision 03 calls frontage debated (`03-formations-tactics.md:13`). No variant moved it.
- The durations in bands 1 and 6 are those of armies with neighbours and lines behind. The patch is two centuries with open flanks.
- Who broke first is in every record (`metrics.rs:174`) and in no section. At the centres the Romans lost 14 of 16 (`priors-field.md:28-29`) and 26 to 29 of 30 (`preregistration.md:772`). In the replayed sample the Roman body broke after 81 % of its file leaders' falls and the warband after 26 % of its own (`docs/slice0/scored/leaders.txt:6-7, 13-14`). M2 expects the Romans to win most runs (`07-validation.md:65`).

Do: add the loser's share per cell. In a second round sample frontage, depth and opening distance as inputs (`07-validation.md:29-34`), and add a line whose flanks are closed.

### P7. Conduct: the experiment varied the two things that did not matter and held fixed what did.

- It could compare substrates and memories. It could not localize a fault in what the cells share. After the merge the adversarial apparatus guarded an axis on which nothing was at stake.
- The gate did not stop the batch. Every cell read "cannot pass" after `base`, and 56 of 64 steps still ran, about six of the seven days (`preregistration.md:462-473`; `docs/slice0/report/07-checks.md:157-159`).
- Relief was known at the freeze to need a line that lasts (`preregistration.md:353-358`). Where it came, "no effect" (`05-relief.md:81-84`) is what a model with nothing slow to restore must give. It is no evidence on S4 or M8.
- The ladder is not nested between rungs 2 and 3 (`preregistration.md:93-94`), so "rung 3 shows less" compares two priors.
- The pairwise speed reading is 11 % under its line, from one run with an unexplained load of 1.7 to 1.9 (`docs/slice0/report/08-speed.md:18-22`). I would not call that reading final. I still expect "under" once the man is added. The field's sevenfold miss is safe. On both the cost is the perception the thin man needs (`08-speed.md:35-39`).
- The leaks the report names carried one fact, that nothing passed. They do not weaken the verdict.
