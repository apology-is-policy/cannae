# 10. What Slice 0 does not answer

**Draft by Paullus, 2026-11-19.** The first four were listed in [the outline](../report-outline.md)
before the batch ended. The rest were added with the results, and each says which section
it comes from.

## Listed beforehand

- **Whether a formed line shoves.** A man in his place does not press in Slice 0, on either
  engine. That is put off to Slice 1, not settled.
- **Fear of missiles that fell nobody.** In `volleys` men see only the falls the fire causes
  (section 4).
- **The shape of the hazard of breaking.** No source gives it, so it is reported (section 3:
  a median Weibull shape of 1.02 to 1.42 on the field engine and 1.52 to 1.67 on the
  pairwise) and no rung is failed on it.
- **Any second round.** Nothing is retuned under this pre-registration. A second round would
  be a new one, with its own priors and its own freeze.

## Added with the results

- **Which mechanism is wrong.** Both engines share the man, his fear and his ties, and differ
  in the substrate, and both fail the same way (section 1). That points at what they share;
  it does not prove it.
- **Whether priors outside the frozen ones would pass.** The 200 sets span the priors as
  frozen (section 1).
- **Whether relief helps.** The drill was allowed only after twenty minutes, and nearly every
  line had broken by five, so it came in 1 to 4 % of the stride's runs. Where it came, it
  showed no effect that the twins could see (section 5).
- **Whether a flank attack makes a line fare worse.** The band charges at ten minutes, after
  nearly every break, and the scorer measured the leg with a clock started about five seconds
  early (section 6).
- **Whether rung 3 shows the drill more than rung 2.** It cannot be told on either engine
  (section 5).
- **The pairwise engine's speed with the perception on all threads.** The second reading has
  not been taken (section 8).
- **How the discs' passage fault acts in a battle.** It had one jammed relief to act on at
  each of the pairwise engine's rungs 0 and 1, and none at rungs 2 and 3; the remedy was not
  built, so what it did cannot be shown (section 5).
